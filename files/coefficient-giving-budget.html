Trustworthy Biomedical AI Reading Group and Evaluation Sprint

Budget summary prepared by Sam Guessoum for a 3-month capacity-building pilot.

Project Scope

This pilot would support a small student-led program at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, connected to Sam Guessoum's research network at Massachusetts Eye and Ear / Harvard Medical School. The program would help early-career AI and biomedical-AI researchers build practical capacity in trustworthy AI evaluation, uncertainty-aware systems, evidence-grounded decision support, and responsible deployment in high-stakes settings.

Funding Request

Total request: USD 10,000 for a 3-month pilot.

Budget

Budget Line Amount
Participant mini-awards or project prizes USD 3,000
Speaker honoraria for 2-4 invited sessions USD 2,000
Food and event costs for weekly meetings and sprint days USD 2,000
Website, materials, and communication costs USD 500
Travel or local collaboration support USD 1,500
Administrative contingency USD 1,000
Total USD 10,000

Notes

The pilot would use public literature, public or synthetic examples, and non-sensitive educational materials. It would not require sharing restricted clinical datasets or confidential institutional information.
